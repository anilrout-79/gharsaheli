📚 Investment Basics

Mutual Fund = kai logon ka paisa milake stocks/bonds mein invest. Professional manage karta hai. SIP se shuru kar sakte ho.
SIP = har mahine fixed amount invest. Example: ₹500/month × 20 yrs @12% ≈ ₹5L+ value. Discipline + compounding.
Digital gold, Gold ETF, SGB (Sovereign Gold Bonds). Jewellery mein making charges zyada — investment ke liye digital better.
3–6 mahine ka kharcha liquid account mein. Job loss, medical, ya sudden expense ke liye — loans se bachati hai.
Health cover pehle (₹5L+ family floater). Life cover = 10–15× annual income. Term plan sasta aur enough.

🎓 Tax ka Gyaan

CA ki zaroorat nahi. Bas itna kaafi hai.

Agar annual income basic exemption se upar hai (New Regime mein ~₹3L + rebate up to ₹7L), to ITR file karna better hai — refund, loans, visas ke liye.

New Regime (approx):

  • 0–3L: 0%
  • 3–7L: 5% (rebate se often 0 up to 7L)
  • 7–10L: 10%
  • 10–12L: 15%
  • 12–15L: 20%
  • 15L+: 30%
1) incometax.gov.in pe register · 2) Form select (ITR-1 common) · 3) Income + deductions enter · 4) e-Verify (Aadhaar OTP).
80C (PPF, ELSS, life), 80D (health), HRA, home loan interest. Sukanya deposits also under 80C (old regime). New regime mein mostly slab + rebate.
Wrong ITR form, bank interest miss, late filing fee, PAN-Aadhaar not linked, cash deposits unexplained.
Helpline: 1800-103-0025 · incometax.gov.in · e-Filing helpdesk
